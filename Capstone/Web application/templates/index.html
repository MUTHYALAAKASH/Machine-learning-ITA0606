    Scam Signatures & Threat Intelligence Knowledge Base

    Top threat vectors recognized by the natural language feature union.

    Off-Platform Communication

    1. Telegram & WhatsApp Interview Scams

    Recruiters asking applicants to download Telegram or WhatsApp to interview with an untraceable handle (e.g. @HiringManager2026). Used to evade job portal moderation and domain verification.

    Triggers: telegram, whatsapp, messaging app
    Advance Fee / Check Fraud

    2. Counterfeit Cashier Check & Equipment Scams

    The victim is mailed a counterfeit cashier check for $2,000–$4,500 to buy home office equipment from an approved vendor. After wiring funds, the check bounces and the victim is financially liable.

    Triggers: cashier check, deposit check, wire transfer, western union
    Money Mule Operations

    3. Crypto Escrow & Payment Processing

    Advertised as remote financial processing. Victims receive fraudulent transactions into personal bank accounts and convert them to Bitcoin/USDT, leading to account freezes and wire fraud charges.

    Triggers: crypto, bitcoin, usdt, personal bank account
    Phishing & Identity Theft

    4. Immediate Hiring Without Interview

    High hourly pay ($45-$60/hr) with "no experience necessary". Victims are pressured to immediately provide Social Security numbers, bank account details, and passport photos prior to an offer.

    Triggers: urgently hiring, no experience required, no interview
